## Getting Started

### Welcome to MoolaBuddy! 🎉

MoolaBuddy is your comprehensive personal finance management app designed to help you track expenses, set goals, and make smarter financial decisions with AI-powered insights.

### First Time Setup

1. **Launch the App**: Open MoolaBuddy on your device
2. **Complete Onboarding**: Follow the guided tour to understand key features
3. **Add Your First Transaction**: Start by recording your income or expenses
4. **Set Up Your Profile**: Customize your financial goals and preferences

### App Navigation

- **Sidebar Menu**: Access all features from the hamburger menu
- **Bottom Navigation**: Quick access to main sections
- **Back Button**: Navigate through your financial journey
- **Search & Filter**: Find specific transactions or data quickly

## Transactions Management

### Adding Transactions

#### **Manual Transaction Entry**
1. Navigate to **Transactions** section
2. Tap **"Add New Transaction"**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Description**: What the transaction was for
   - **Amount**: Enter the exact amount
   - **Category**: Choose or create a category
   - **Type**: Income or Expense
   - **Date**: When the transaction occurred

#### **Transaction Categories**
Common categories include:
- **Income**: Salary, Freelance, Investments
- **Food**: Groceries, Restaurants, Takeout
- **Transport**: Fuel, Public Transport, Maintenance
- **Entertainment**: Movies, Games, Subscriptions
- **Utilities**: Electricity, Water, Internet
- **Shopping**: Clothing, Electronics, Home

### Managing Transactions

#### **Edit Transactions**
1. Find the transaction in your list
2. Tap the **"Edit"** button
3. Modify any details
4. Save changes

#### **Delete Transactions**
1. Find the transaction
2. Tap **"Delete"**
3. Confirm deletion

#### **Transaction History**
- **Filter by Date**: View specific time periods
- **Filter by Category**: See spending patterns
- **Search**: Find specific transactions quickly

### Best Practices

- **Be Consistent**: Use the same category names
- **Add Details**: Include descriptions for better tracking
- **Regular Updates**: Record transactions as they happen
- **Review Monthly**: Check your spending patterns regularly
